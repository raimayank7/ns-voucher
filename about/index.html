About Nivesh Sahayak Voucher

Nivesh Sahayak Voucher is part of a family of free business document tools by Nivesh Sahayak, made for shops, traders, service providers and small businesses in India.

Why we built it

Small businesses need correct bills and documents every day, but most software asks for an account, an internet connection and a monthly fee before you can make even one bill. These tools are made by people who work in accounts and GST every day, so the documents follow the rules, and you can start in seconds.

How it is different

  • No login. Open the page and start.
  • Your data stays on your device. Nothing you type is sent to us.
  • Works offline once opened, on phones and computers.
  • Made for India: GST rules, Indian number style (lakh, crore), UPI and labels in Hindi and Punjabi.
  • Enter once, use everywhere: your businesses, customers and items are shared by every Nivesh Sahayak document tool on this device.
